Who I Am

I have seven years of experience teaching high school science and hold a master’s degree in Curriculum and Instruction from the University of Cincinnati. Studying engineering and science education taught me to bring technical problem-solving and scientific reasoning into the way I design instruction.

My work in the classroom informs my interests in curriculum development, instructional design, educational research, and emerging technologies.

Academic & Professional Journey

  1. University of Akron

    I studied chemical engineering for three years, developing a foundation in technical problem-solving.

  2. University of Cincinnati

    I transferred to pursue Secondary Education in Integrated Science with a focus on Chemistry.

  3. High School Science Teaching

    I’ve taught Physical Science, Chemistry, and Oceanography. I design lessons for a range of learning needs and look for ways to make complex science accessible and relevant.

  4. Graduate Study & Action Research

    I earned a master’s degree in Curriculum and Instruction from the University of Cincinnati and studied how AI could support reflection in secondary STEM learning.

My Approach to Education

I believe students learn more meaningfully when they can connect classroom content to situations they recognize. I use projects to give students practice thinking critically, solving problems, and applying what they know beyond a traditional assessment.

I use technology and adapt instruction to support different learning needs while giving students room to be curious, independent, and creative. I regularly review how lessons went and revise my materials to better support learning.

Research & Innovation

In my master’s action research, I explored how secondary STEM students could use AI to check their reasoning and reflect on their understanding. That work deepened my interest in responsible AI integration, educational research, and using data to make instructional decisions.

I continue to explore where emerging technologies can improve learning materials, make instructional work more efficient, and address specific problems educators face.

Leadership & Collaboration

As a high school esports coach, I support students outside the classroom and build skills in team management, technology troubleshooting, and program organization.

I also serve on my school’s Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS) Tier 2 committee. I collaborate with other educators on targeted strategies that support student behavior, well-being, and success. Both roles give me opportunities to adapt, work with others, and grow as an educator.

Looking Ahead

I want to keep designing meaningful, accessible learning experiences. I’m interested in opportunities that connect my background in science and education with curriculum development, instructional design, research, and technology. Across settings, I want my work to help people learn and explore better ways to address challenges in education.
